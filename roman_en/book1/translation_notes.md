# Verridia — Book One Translation Notes

This file records unresolved or editorially significant translation decisions. It is not part of the novel manuscript.

## Final terminology decisions

- **Kor-Ateşler → the Ember-Fires.** This rendering preserves both the ember and fire imagery without adding new lore. Singular member: **an Ember-Fire**.
- **Sarmaşan → the Sarmaşan / the Sarmaşan River.** The proper name is retained; “River” is added where English clarity requires it.
- **Anya Ana → Mother Anya.** Used as a community honorific rather than a personal-name change.
- **Bozkır → Bozkır.** The horse's proper name is retained, including its Turkish diacritic, because the guide only mandates semantic translation for transparent geographic and institutional names.
- **Boğucu Ana → the Drowning Mother.** This deity name is not locked in the guide. “Drowning” carries the sea cult's dual sense of the ocean as giver and taker of life. The flagship is therefore *Oath of the Drowning Mother*.
- **Kuzey Sefer Yolu → the Northern Campaign Road.** Used as a proper route name.
- **Sis-İşçileri → the Mist Workers.** Treated as a named dock-labor group.
- **Çatlak Kurukafa → the Cracked Skull.** Treated as a tavern name.
- **Paslı Finn → Rusty Finn.** The personal name remains Finn; the descriptive epithet is translated in natural English.
- **Eskiler → the Ancients.** Capitalized when the word identifies the old civilization rather than merely ancient people or objects.
- **Tek-Kol Roric → One-Armed Roric.** The physical epithet is translated; Roric's personal name remains unchanged.
- **Gelgit Lejyonları → the Tide Legions.** Treated as the formal name of the Hegemony military formation.
- **Karanlık Ay → the Dark Month; Beklenti Yılı → the Year of Waiting.** These renderings preserve their formal calendar status.
